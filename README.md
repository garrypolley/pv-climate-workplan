# Prairie Village climate actions

The page opens with the Environmental Committee workshop’s placement of the work: 2027 Opportunities, 2028 Budget, 2029/2030 Planning, and 2030+ Awareness.

The rest of the page is how the group got there. It reads a 45-row action table against the City of Prairie Village [Community Climate Action Guide](https://www.prairievillageks.gov/DocumentCenter/View/602/Community-Climate-Action-Guide-PDF) (November 2025), then the four-question feasibility scale, the six-box prioritization matrix, the group’s impact-and-feasibility chart, the sheet that files actions into those six boxes, an earlier reading of the four years, and the first photograph of stickers. The years at the top are the later photograph.

The guide’s chapter “Prairie Village’s Role in Climate Action” lists 38 city actions with codes EL, EE, T, and W. The table’s Source column uses those codes. Each code appears on exactly one row. Twenty-two other rows cite the Environmental Committee and are not in the guide.

The table was transcribed from photographs. The sheet shows no title, date, or adoption line. Guide wording comes from the city’s PDF.
